Debunking Lore

Hair Care: Myth vs Scientific Fact

MYTH #1

"Frequent washing ruins your hair and causes oiliness."

(Tap to flip for the truth)

FACT

Washing with sulfate-free cleansers as often as needed removes oxidized sebum that clogs follicles. Only harsh detergents trigger rebound oiliness!

MYTH #2

"Trimming ends makes hair grow faster from the root."

(Tap to flip for the truth)

FACT

Growth happens exclusively at the dermal papilla in the scalp. Trims simply stop split ends from traveling up, preserving length retention!

MYTH #3

"Applying pure raw oil provides moisture to dry hair."

(Tap to flip for the truth)

FACT

Oils are hydrophobic lipids—they seal moisture in, but contain zero water! Always apply hydration (water/leave-in) first, then seal with oil.

MYTH #4

"Sulfate-free shampoos cannot lather or clean thoroughly."

(Tap to flip for the truth)

FACT

Modern coconut isethionate surfactants produce luxurious, micro-dense lather that traps pollution without stripping natural 18-MEA lipids.

Why are sulfates harmful to color-treated and curly hair? +

Sulfates (SLS and SLES) have an aggressive ionic charge that strips the natural lipid membrane from the cuticle. In color-treated hair, this allows dye molecules to rinse out immediately. In curly hair, it robs spirals of moisture, resulting in chronic frizz, breakage, and loss of definition.

Trichology Lab Method

The 3-Minute Water Glass Test

How to test your hair cuticle porosity at home using a simple glass of water.

  1. Step 1: Take a clean, shed strand of dry hair (brush from a clean scalp without heavy styling products).
  2. Step 2: Drop the strand into a clear glass filled with room-temperature tap water.
  3. Step 3: Watch what happens after 2 to 4 minutes:
Floats on top of water: Low Porosity (Tightly shut cuticles that repel hydration).
Floats in the middle: Medium Porosity (Optimal, balanced cuticle scales).
Sinks quickly to bottom: High Porosity (Porous, raised cuticles that drink water instantly).
